# Skedence Demo Call Guide
## 15-Minute Demo Structure

**Purpose:** Show the value of Skedence quickly and clearly, address concerns, and move toward a trial signup.

---

## Pre-Demo Preparation (5 minutes before call)

### Have Open and Ready:
- [ ] Admin portal (logged into demo account)
- [ ] Client app on iPhone (demo mode)
- [ ] Trainer app on iPhone (demo mode)
- [ ] Pricing page or PDF
- [ ] Calendar for booking follow-up
- [ ] Their website/social media (research their business)

### Know About the Prospect:
- Number of trainers
- Current booking method
- Payment processing method
- Biggest pain point (if known)
- Type of training (1-on-1, classes, hybrid)

---

## Demo Structure (15 minutes)

### 1. Introduction (1-2 minutes)

**Script:**
> "Thanks for taking the time to chat! I know you're busy, so I'll keep this focused. I pulled up your [website/Instagram] - looks like you're doing [specific observation about their business]. Before I jump into the demo, can you tell me quickly what your biggest challenge is with managing your training business right now?"

**Listen for:**
- Scheduling hassles
- Payment tracking
- Time spent on admin
- Client communication
- Growing the team

**Transition:**
> "Got it. Well, let me show you exactly how Skedence solves that..."

---

### 2. Admin Portal Overview (5 minutes)

#### Dashboard (30 seconds)
**Show:** Main dashboard
> "This is what you see when you log in. Quick view of today's schedule, recent bookings, and key metrics. Everything at a glance."

#### Bookings/Schedule (1 minute)
**Show:** Calendar view or bookings list
> "Here's where all your appointments live. You can see upcoming, past, filter by trainer, by client, by type. Watch this - I can reschedule by dragging and dropping [demonstrate]. The client gets notified automatically."

#### Clients (1 minute)
**Show:** Client directory, click into one profile
> "All your clients in one place. Click any client and you see their complete history - past appointments, packages they've purchased, how many sessions they have left, payment history. No more 'How many sessions do I have left?' texts."

#### Packages (1 minute)
**Show:** Package management page
> "This is where most trainers increase their revenue. Create any pricing package you want - 5 sessions, 10 sessions, monthly memberships. Set expiration dates, pricing, and it all tracks automatically. Clients can buy these right through the app."

#### Reports (1 minute)
**Show:** Revenue report or appointments analytics
> "The analytics are powerful. See exactly where your revenue comes from - which packages sell best, which trainers book the most, peak times, everything. You can export any report to CSV for your accountant."

**Pause for questions**

---

### 3. Client Mobile App (3 minutes)

**Show:** iPhone with client app open (screen share or hold up to camera)

#### Booking Flow (1.5 minutes)
> "Now here's what your clients see. Let me book a session..."
- Open app
- Show trainer selection (if applicable)
- Browse available times
- Select slot
- Add to cart
- Complete booking (demo mode)

> "That's it. Your clients can do this at 11 PM on a Sunday. The booking appears on your calendar instantly, and you both get confirmation."

#### Packages (1 minute)
> "Clients can also purchase packages directly from the app."
- Show packages screen
- Show "Buy" flow
- Show Apple Pay integration

> "Apple Pay makes it one-click. Way easier than sending Venmo requests back and forth."

#### Profile & History (30 seconds)
> "They can see their upcoming appointments, past history, and how many sessions they have left in any package."

**Pause for questions**

---

### 4. Trainer Mobile App (2 minutes)

**Show:** iPhone with admin app

> "And here's your app as the trainer..."
- Show today's schedule
- Show client lookup
- Show ability to add notes after session
- Show notifications for new bookings

> "Everything you need on the go. Most trainers use the web portal for setup and management, then the app for day-to-day."

---

### 5. Key Features Rapid-Fire (1 minute)

> "Let me quickly highlight a few more things:"
- ✅ Stripe integration - secure payment processing
- ✅ Digital waivers - clients sign before first session
- ✅ Multi-trainer support - each gets their own calendar
- ✅ Automatic reminders - reduce no-shows (coming soon)
- ✅ Customizable policies - cancellations, booking windows, etc.

---

### 6. Pricing & Closing (2-3 minutes)

**Present Pricing:**
> "So pricing is straightforward: $49 per month, everything included. No setup fees, no contracts, cancel anytime. The only other cost is Stripe's processing fees - 2.9% plus 30 cents per transaction, which is standard."

> "We also offer a 14-day free trial, no credit card required. You can set up your whole business, test it with a few clients, and make sure it works for you before paying anything."

**Address Concerns:**

**If they hesitate:**
> "What questions do you have?"

**Common concerns & responses:**

**"Seems complicated to set up"**
> "Most people are live and taking bookings within an hour. And we offer a complimentary onboarding call where we walk through setup with you."

**"Will my clients use the app?"**
> "They can book on the web too, but we find over 80% prefer the app once they try it. It's as easy as using Instagram."

**"I already use [other tool]"**
> "I get it. The difference is Skedence brings everything together - you're not juggling 3-4 different tools. And we can help you import your existing clients."

**"Need to think about it"**
> "Of course! How about this - I'll send you a login to a demo account so you can click around yourself. And I'll include that one-page overview PDF. Does it make sense to reconnect in a few days?"

**Ask for Next Step:**
> "Based on what you've seen, do you want to try the free trial? I can send you a signup link right now, and I'm happy to jump on a quick call when you're setting up if you need help."

## After the Demo

### If They Say Yes:
1. Send signup link immediately
2. Send welcome email with:
   - Getting started checklist
   - Link to schedule onboarding call
   - Your direct contact info
3. Follow up in 24 hours to check on progress
4. Schedule onboarding call if needed

### If They Need to Think:
1. Send recap email with:
   - Demo recording (if recorded)
   - One-page overview PDF
   - Pricing breakdown
   - Case study or testimonial (if available)
2. Schedule follow-up call/email for 3-5 days
3. Add to nurture sequence

### If They Say No:
1. Ask why (learn for future demos)
2. Offer to stay in touch
3. Send occasional value emails (tips, updates)
4. Check back in 3-6 months

---

## Demo Tips for Success

### Do:
✅ Let them talk - learn about their business
✅ Customize demo to their pain points
✅ Show real value, not every feature
✅ Use their terminology (if they say "sessions" not "appointments")
✅ Be enthusiastic but not pushy
✅ Have smooth transitions between sections
✅ Know your demo data (don't fumble with test accounts)

### Don't:
❌ Rush through screens too fast
❌ Show bugs or errors (know your demo account!)
❌ Talk too much without pausing
❌ Ignore their questions
❌ Bad-mouth competitors
❌ Over-promise features not yet built
❌ Skip asking for the next step

## Handling Objections

### "Too expensive"
**Response:** "I understand. Let me break it down - at $49/month, if this saves you even 10 hours per month, that's paying yourself $4.90/hour to do admin. What do you charge for an hour of training? Probably $50-100+. This lets you train more and admin less."

### "I need to talk to my partner/business partner"
**Response:** "Makes sense. Would it help if they joined a quick call? Or I can send you materials to share. What concerns do you think they'll have?"

### "I'm not tech-savvy"
**Response:** "That's exactly why we built it this way. If you can use Instagram or iMessage, you can use Skedence. And we'll help you set it up - most people are amazed at how simple it is."

### "My clients won't use an app"
**Response:** "That's what everyone thinks! But once clients see how easy it is - easier than texting back and forth - they love it. Plus, they can book on the web if they prefer. The app is optional but most choose it."

### "What if I want to cancel?"
**Response:** "Cancel anytime, no questions asked. We don't lock you into contracts because we want to earn your business every month. You can export all your data before you leave."

### "I don't have many clients yet"
**Response:** "Perfect time to start with a system that grows with you. You'll look professional from day one, and you won't have to migrate later when you're busy. Plus, the automated booking helps you get more clients."

## Demo Account Setup

### Prepare Your Demo Account With:
- 10-15 sample clients with realistic names
- Mix of upcoming and past appointments
- 3-4 sample pricing packages
- At least one completed payment
- Some sample data in reports
- A few trainers if showing multi-trainer features
- Digital waiver example

### Keep Demo Data Clean:
- No test/dummy obvious names
- Realistic pricing
- Accurate dates (nothing from 2020!)
- Professional profile photos
- Complete information (not half-filled forms)

---

## Success Metrics

Track these for each demo:
- [ ] Demo completed (yes/no)
- [ ] Length of demo (target: 15-20 min)
- [ ] Questions asked by prospect
- [ ] Main objections raised
- [ ] Outcome (trial signup, follow-up scheduled, not interested)
- [ ] Follow-up needed (yes/no)

Review monthly to improve demo flow and conversion rate.

---

**Goal:** Every demo should either result in a trial signup or a scheduled follow-up. Never end a demo without a next step agreed upon.
