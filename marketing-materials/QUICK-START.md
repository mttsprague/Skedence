## 🚀 Quick Start (5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Create PDFs
1. Open `sales-deck.html` in Chrome/Safari/Firefox
2. Press **Cmd+P** (Mac) or **Ctrl+P** (Windows)
3. Choose "Save as PDF"
4. **Turn on "Background graphics"** (important!)
5. Save as `Skedence-Sales-Deck.pdf`

Repeat for `one-page-overview.html` → Save as `Skedence-Overview.pdf`

### Step 2: Send Your First Email
1. Open `email-campaign-1-introduction.html` in browser
2. Select all (Cmd+A / Ctrl+A)
3. Copy and paste into Gmail/Outlook
4. Personalize:
   - Replace "Hi there" with their name
   - Add how you found them
   - Update signature
5. Send!

### Step 3: Book First Demo
When someone responds:
1. Open `demo-call-guide.md`
2. Review the 15-minute structure
3. Have admin portal open
4. Follow the script

## 💼 Best Practices

### For Cold Outreach:
1. Research them first (website, Instagram)
2. Reference something specific about their business
3. Keep first email short (use campaign-1)
4. Don't attach large files initially
5. Follow up 2-3 times before giving up

### For Warm Leads:
1. Send comprehensive materials immediately
2. Offer demo within 24-48 hours
3. Be responsive - speed matters
4. Customize demo to their pain points

### For In-Person Meetings:
1. Bring printed sales deck
2. Have iPad/laptop ready for live demo
3. Leave one-page overview as handout
4. Follow up same day with email + PDFs

## 🎬 Creating Your Demo Video

You have three options:

### Option 1: DIY (Free, 2-4 hours)
1. Use `video-script.md` as your guide
2. Record screen with QuickTime (Mac) or OBS (free)
3. Record voiceover separately
4. Edit in iMovie (Mac) or DaVinci Resolve (free)
5. Add background music from YouTube Audio Library

### Option 2: Simple Screen Recording (30 minutes)
1. Use Loom or Vidyard
2. Record yourself walking through the admin portal
3. Follow the video script structure
4. Less polished but authentic and quick

### Option 3: Professional ($200-$2,000)
1. Hire on Fiverr or Upwork
2. Provide video script and screen recordings
3. They handle editing, music, graphics
4. Get polished result in 1-2 weeks

## 🆘 Need Help?

### Common Issues:

**"PDFs don't look right"**
→ Make sure "Background graphics" is enabled when printing

**"Email HTML doesn't work in my email client"**
→ Try copying into Gmail web version, or send as PDF attachment

**"I don't know who to reach out to"**
→ Start with: Personal trainers on Instagram, local gym owners, fitness coaches on LinkedIn

**"Demo felt too long"**
→ Focus on THEIR pain points only, skip features they don't care about

**"Not getting responses to emails"**
→ Try different subject lines, send at different times, personalize more

## 🎓 Pro Tips from Successful Launches

1. **Personalization beats perfection** - A customized simple email beats a perfect generic one

2. **Speed matters** - Respond to inquiries within 1 hour (85% higher conversion)

3. **Social proof wins** - Share success stories and metrics constantly

4. **Follow up more than you think** - Average 5-7 touchpoints needed to convert

5. **Demo without pressure** - Let them talk more than you, listen to their problems

6. **Start with your network** - Friends, family, existing clients - easier first customers

7. **Content before promotion** - Give value on social media before asking for business

8. **Track everything** - You can't improve what you don't measure
