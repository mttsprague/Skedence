Why Connect Stripe?

Stripe is your payment processor. When clients purchase lesson packages through the Skedence app, the money goes directly into your Stripe account (minus a small 3% platform fee). You'll need a Stripe account to accept payments and run your business.

Pro Tips

  • •Stripe is free to set up - you only pay per transaction (2.9% + 30¢)
  • •Money typically arrives in your bank account within 2 business days
  • •All payment data is encrypted and PCI compliant
  • •You can view all transactions in your Stripe dashboard

How to Connect Stripe

Setting up Stripe takes about 5 minutes and requires your business information and bank account details.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. 1Navigate to **Business Settings → Stripe** in the left sidebar
  2. 2Click the **"Connect Stripe Account"** button
  3. 3You'll be redirected to Stripe's secure website
  4. 4Choose **"Create a new account"** (or sign in if you already have one)
  5. 5Fill in your business information: business name, type, address
  6. 6Add your personal details: name, date of birth, SSN (for identity verification)
  7. 7Enter your bank account information where you want to receive payments
  8. 8Review and submit for approval
  9. 9Stripe will verify your information (usually instant, sometimes 1-2 days)
  10. 10Once approved, you'll be redirected back to Skedence
  11. 11You'll see a ✅ green checkmark confirming your account is connected

Pro Tips

  • •Have your EIN or SSN ready for faster setup
  • •Use your business bank account, not personal
  • •Make sure your business name matches your legal documents
  • •You can update your bank account later in the Stripe dashboard

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I don't have a business entity yet?
You can use your personal information and select "Individual" as your business type. You can always upgrade to a business entity later.
How much does Stripe charge?
Stripe charges 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction. Skedence charges an additional 3% platform fee. For example, if a client pays $100, Stripe takes $3.20, Skedence takes $3, and you receive $93.80.
When will I receive payments?
Stripe deposits funds to your bank account on a rolling 2-day basis. For example, payments from Monday arrive Wednesday, Tuesday arrives Thursday, etc.
Can I change my connected account later?
Yes, but it requires disconnecting your current account first. Contact support for assistance with this process.
