About CSV Exports

  • • All exports are in CSV (Comma-Separated Values) format
  • • Files can be opened in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet application
  • • Exports include all historical data from your organization
  • • Data is exported as of the current date and time
  • • Sensitive information like passwords and payment details are never included
