Our Story

Skedence was born from a simple frustration: managing a coaching business shouldn't require juggling multiple tools, spreadsheets, and endless text messages with clients.

As former fitness trainers ourselves, we experienced firsthand the chaos of manual scheduling, payment tracking, and client management. We saw talented coaches spending more time on admin work than actually coaching their clients.

We built Skedence to solve this problem—a single platform where coaches can manage everything: bookings, payments, client relationships, and business analytics. No more switching between apps. No more missed appointments. Just more time doing what you love: coaching.

Our Mission

To empower every coach and trainer with simple, powerful software that eliminates administrative headaches and helps them focus on what matters most—changing lives through coaching.
