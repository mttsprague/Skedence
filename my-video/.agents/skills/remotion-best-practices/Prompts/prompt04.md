# The Journey - Skedence How It Works Video (Agent-Designed)

**Concept:** A fast-paced, story-driven narrative that follows the complete user journey through Skedence. Professional, sleek, and energetic.

**Duration:** ~55 seconds  
**Style:** Dark background, orange accents, dynamic transitions, modern glassmorphism effects  
**Tone:** Confident, efficient, professional

---

## Scene Structure

### Scene 1: Opening Hook (5s)
**Visual:** Bold, centered headline with subtle pulse animation  
**Copy:** "Your training business, simplified"  
**Effect:** Confident opener, sets professional tone

### Scene 2: Quick Setup (8s)
**Visual:** Web dashboard with packages and availability screens fading in  
**Copy:** "Set up your business in minutes"  
**Subtext:** "Packages • Availability • Pricing" (orange)  
**Effect:** Shows how fast and easy setup is

### Scene 3: Athlete Discovery (7s)
**Visual:** Client mobile app showing trainer profiles  
**Copy:** "Athletes find you instantly"  
**Effect:** Clean mobile frame showcasing discovery experience

### Scene 4: The Booking (8s)
**Visual:** Two phones side-by-side - athlete booking, trainer receiving  
**Copy:** "Book and confirm in seconds"  
**Effect:** Shows the seamless connection between both sides

### Scene 5: Staying Synced (7s)
**Visual:** Both apps showing lesson details simultaneously  
**Copy:** "Everyone stays in sync"  
**Effect:** Emphasizes automatic updates across the platform

### Scene 6: Business Insights (8s)
**Visual:** Revenue dashboard with charts and analytics  
**Copy:** "Track your growth in real-time"  
**Effect:** Shows the business intelligence side

### Scene 7: Scale Effortlessly (7s)
**Visual:** Three-panel view (web + trainer app + client app)  
**Copy:** "Scale without the chaos"  
**Subtext:** "One system for your entire training business" (orange)  
**Effect:** Shows the complete system working together

### Scene 8: Closing (5s)
**Visual:** Skedence logo with orange glow (reused from HowItWorks)  
**Copy:** "Run your training business like a business"  
**Effect:** Strong, memorable close matching HowItWorks ending

---

## Design Elements

- **Typography:** Inter font family, weights 400-700
- **Colors:** 
  - Background: #0f1115 (dark)
  - Accent: #ff6b35 (orange)
  - Text: #ffffff (white)
- **Animations:** Smooth fade-ins, scale effects, subtle parallax
- **Effects:** Glassmorphism on overlays, vignettes for depth
- **Pacing:** Fast but readable, builds momentum throughout

---

## Key Differentiators from Prompt03

1. **Story-driven:** Follows actual user journey instead of feature list
2. **Faster pacing:** 55s vs 80s - more dynamic
3. **Dual perspective:** Shows both trainer and athlete experiences simultaneously
4. **Emotional arc:** Setup → Discovery → Connection → Growth → Scale
5. **Tighter narrative:** Each scene builds on the previous one
