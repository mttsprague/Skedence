🎬 STRUCTURE

Create a 4-act narrative structure.

ACT 1 — THE PROBLEM

Scene: Dark background.
Subtle floating blurred text messages appear behind.

Text messages examples (generic, not UI screenshots):

“Are you free Tuesday?”

“Can we reschedule?”

“Did you get Venmo?”

“How many sessions left?”

These should feel layered and slightly chaotic.

Camera motion:

Slight handheld micro movement

Very subtle shake

Slow push in

Headline text fades in center:

“Still running your training like this?”

Pause.

Then:

“Text messages.”
“Venmo.”
“Spreadsheets.”
“Calendar apps.”

Each word appears sequentially.

Tone: Slight tension.

ACT 2 — THE SHIFT

Hard cut.
All chaos disappears instantly.

Dark clean background.

Scheduling dashboard screenshot animates in from center with smooth scale up (90% → 100%).

Soft blue glow around edges.

Camera slowly pushes toward the time slots.

Headline:

“Everything.”
Pause.
“In one place.”

Minimal.

Let the UI breathe.

Then transition smoothly to revenue screenshot.

Slow horizontal pan across revenue metrics.

Headline:

“Your schedule.”
“Your payments.”
“Your revenue.”

Each line fades in one at a time.

Tone: Controlled. Stable.

ACT 3 — THE POWER

Transition to third screenshot (Packages or Trainers).

Slight parallax effect.
UI slightly elevated.

Headline:

“Structure.”
“Control.”
“Visibility.”

Then:

“All in one dashboard.”

Camera gently zooms out to show entire card layout.

Optional subtle animated highlight around key metrics (glow pulse, not flashy).

ACT 4 — THE IDENTITY SHIFT

Fade to dark.

Split screen:

Left side:
Blurred chaos background (very subtle).

Right side:
Clean dashboard card.

Headline:

“Side hustle.”
Pause.
“Business.”

Pause.

Final frame:
Skedence logo centered.

Under logo:

“Run it like a business.”

No extra effects.

Hold long enough for impact.
