Skedence — “How It Works” Product Video

Create a modern SaaS product walkthrough video explaining how the Skedence platform works for private training businesses.

The platform includes three components:

• Web dashboard (business management)
• Trainer app (trainer schedule management)
• Client app (lesson booking for athletes/parents)

The goal of the video is to clearly show how these three systems work together.

Brand Rules

The product name must always be spelled Skedence exactly.

Never auto-correct or alter the spelling.

If the AI generator cannot guarantee correct spelling, use the Skedence logo image instead of typed text.

Tone and Style

The video should feel like a professional SaaS product explainer similar to companies like Stripe, Linear, or Notion.

Visual style:

• modern
• minimal
• clean
• dark SaaS aesthetic
• subtle motion
• smooth transitions

Avoid:

• flashy animations
• loud graphics
• marketing hype

Focus on clarity of the workflow.

Video Length

Target length: 60–75 seconds

Each scene should use slow zooms, gentle pans, and soft UI highlights.

Screenshots should appear as floating UI cards with subtle shadows on a dark background.
