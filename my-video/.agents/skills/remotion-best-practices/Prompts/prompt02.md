🎥 Video #2: Volleyball-Specific Outbound Video

(45–60 seconds)

This is what you send in cold email / DMs.

Tone:
Direct. Specific. Calm. Professional.
No fluff. No hype music.

🎬 STRUCTURE (Follow This Exactly)
SCENE 1 — Direct Hook (0–5s)

Dark background.
Subtle volleyball court lines faint in background.

Text on screen:

“Private Volleyball Coaches —”

Pause 1 second.

“Still scheduling through text?”

Let that breathe.

SCENE 2 — The Real Situation (5–15s)

Quick overlay of fake text messages:

“Are you free Tuesday?”
“Can we move to 6:30?”
“Did you get Venmo?”
“How many sessions left?”

Then overlay:

“At 5 athletes, it works.”

Pause.

“At 15+, it gets messy.”

No exaggeration. Calm delivery.

SCENE 3 — The System (15–35s)

Cut cleanly to your scheduling dashboard screenshot.

Slow cinematic push-in.

Text:

“Set your availability once.”

Cut to packages page (if available).

“Sell lesson packages.”

Cut to revenue page.

“Track revenue in one place.”

No extra words.

Let the UI speak.

SCENE 4 — The Revenue Reality (35–50s)

Dark background again.

Text:

“12 athletes.”
“8 sessions per month.”
“$85 per lesson.”

Revenue number animates:

“$8,160 / month”

Pause.

“You’re managing that through text?”

This is the moment.

This hits identity.

SCENE 5 — Close (50–60s)

Back to dashboard screenshot.

Text:

“Skedence is built for private training businesses.”

Fade to logo.

“Run your lessons like a business.”

End clean.

No hype.
No dramatic music.
Just confidence.

Why This Works for Outbound

When you send this with:

“Made this quick overview for volleyball coaches.”

It feels intentional.

It feels built for them.

It increases:

Curiosity

Replies

Demo bookings

Important: Keep It Focused on Volleyball Language

Use phrases like:

“Private volleyball lessons”

“Club prep”

“Tryout season”

“Position training”

“Serve receive sessions”

Even subtle specificity increases resonance.
