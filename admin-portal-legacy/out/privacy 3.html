Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 24, 2026

Skedence ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

From Business Owners (Admin Users)

When you create a business account, we collect:

  • Name, email address, and password
  • Business name and contact information
  • Phone number and business address
  • Payment information (processed by Stripe, not stored by us)
  • Stripe account ID for payment processing

From Clients (End Users)

When clients use your Skedence-powered booking system, we collect:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Profile information (age, experience level, etc.)
  • Booking history and session attendance
  • Payment information (processed by Stripe, not stored by us)
  • Communication preferences

Automatically Collected Data

  • Device type and operating system
  • IP address and location (approximate)
  • Usage data (pages visited, features used)
  • Error logs and crash reports

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the Service: Process bookings, manage schedules, and facilitate payments
  • Account Management: Create and maintain your account
  • Communication: Send booking confirmations, reminders, and service updates
  • Payment Processing: Facilitate secure payments through Stripe
  • Improve the Platform: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features
  • Customer Support: Respond to inquiries and resolve issues
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with laws and prevent fraud

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do NOT sell your data. We only share data with trusted service providers necessary to operate Skedence:

Service Provider Purpose Data Shared
Stripe Payment processing Payment details, customer names, email addresses
Firebase (Google) Cloud infrastructure, database, authentication All platform data (encrypted and secured)
Apple App Store App distribution and updates Device identifiers, app usage metrics
Important: Skedence does NOT store credit card numbers or bank account details. All payment information is securely handled by Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment processor.

4. Data Security

We take data security seriously and implement industry-standard protections:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit (SSL/TLS) and at rest
  • Firebase Security Rules: Database access is restricted to authorized users only
  • Authentication: Secure login with Firebase Authentication
  • Regular Updates: We keep our systems updated with the latest security patches
  • Access Controls: Only authorized personnel can access server infrastructure

5. Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: We retain your data as long as your account is active
  • After Cancellation: Data is retained for 90 days to allow for reactivation
  • Permanent Deletion: After 90 days, all data is permanently deleted
  • Legal Requirements: Some data may be retained longer if required by law
  • Data Export: You can export your data at any time before deletion

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Export: Download your data in a portable format
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails
  • Data Portability: Transfer your data to another service

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@skedence.com

7. Children's Privacy

Skedence is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

Parent/Guardian Accounts: Parents or guardians may create accounts on behalf of minors for booking training sessions. In such cases, the parent/guardian is responsible for the information provided.

8. Cookies and Tracking

Skedence uses minimal cookies and tracking technologies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for login and authentication
  • Analytics: We may use Firebase Analytics to understand app usage
  • No Third-Party Ads: We do not use advertising cookies or trackers

9. International Data Transfers

Skedence is based in the United States. By using our service, you acknowledge that your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate.

We ensure that all data transfers comply with applicable data protection laws, including GDPR for European users.

10. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know what personal information is collected
  • Right to know if your personal information is sold or disclosed
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data)
  • Right to request deletion of personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

11. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under GDPR:

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to rectification (correction) of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do:

  • The "Last Updated" date will be changed
  • We will notify you via email or in-app notification for significant changes
  • Continued use of Skedence after changes constitutes acceptance

13. Business Responsibility for Client Data

Important: If you are a Business using Skedence, you are a data controller for your clients' personal information. You are responsible for:

  • Obtaining proper consent from your clients to collect and process their data
  • Informing clients about how their data will be used
  • Complying with data protection laws in your jurisdiction
  • Responding to client data requests (access, deletion, etc.)

14. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@skedence.com
  • Support Email: support@skedence.com
